Ground yourself in the repo. Do not use the previous spikes. Read the CRA.md source doc in the spikes/eu-regulations folder and extract Roles, Requirements, Obligations, and Capabilities in full, and store them in JSON format in a cra.json file in the spikes/graph-ingestion3 folder. Do you understand my objective?

Scope: core obligation-bearing provisions only — Article 3 (role definitions), Articles 13-24 (economic-operator and open-source-steward obligations, including the deeming provisions in Art. 21-22 and the traceability duty in Art. 23), and Annex I (essential cybersecurity requirements, Parts I and II). Exclude purely institutional/procedural articles (market surveillance powers, notified bodies, penalties, committee procedure, delegated/implementing act mechanics) unless they impose a direct duty on an economic operator or open-source software steward.

Granularity: extract ALL requirements and ALL obligations — do not cluster multiple distinct duties from different paragraphs into a single Requirement/Obligation pair just because they share an article. Work at the paragraph (or lettered sub-point) level: each numbered paragraph or sub-point that imposes a distinct "shall" duty gets its own Requirement node and its own Obligation node. Merge two paragraphs into one Requirement only in the narrow case where one is a direct cross-reference restating a duty the other already imposes (e.g. an article invoking a specific numbered Annex clause it already names) — and note the merge decision in the _comment field.

It's expected and desired for multiple Obligations, across different Roles, to REQUIRE the same Capability where they draw on the same underlying capacity — that cross-role convergence is the point of the model. It does not justify merging the Obligations themselves: Obligation is 1:1 with its Role (see docs/artifacts/ps-domain-concepts.md), so the same duty text recurring for two different Roles (e.g. importer and distributor both escalating a vulnerability) must be minted as two distinct Obligation nodes, not one shared across HAS edges.
