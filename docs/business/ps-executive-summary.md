# Policy System — Executive Summary

Europe's regulatory wave — CRA, NIS2, GDPR, the AI Act — is shifting from guidance to obligation. Most organizations respond with manual, bolt-on compliance: spreadsheets, consultants, and weeks of review per regulation. The Policy System turns that burden into a competitive advantage: **every regulation instantly understood, mapped to your operations, and verifiably enforced — compliance that is invisible yet inviolable.**

**What it does.** The system structures regulations into a semantic knowledge graph — from regulatory article to obligation, capability, policy, standard, and technical control — so any question (*"Are we compliant with GDPR Article 32?"*) is answered in minutes, with full provenance back to the source text. When regulations change, only the delta is re-processed — not another six-figure review cycle.

**Why customers trust it.** Unlike compliance SaaS, the Policy System runs **entirely in the customer's own environment**. Your governance data never leaves your control; there is no vendor runtime to trust. Curated regulation content arrives as versioned, reviewable updates that you accept on your terms. For organizations whose regulators demand demonstrable control, sovereignty isn't a feature — it's the product.

**Why now.** The Cyber Resilience Act's reporting obligations apply from **11 September 2026** — manufacturers must report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents — with the Act's main obligations applying from **11 December 2027**, enforced through CE marking and market surveillance. The window to build compliance capability before enforcement is now.

**The outcome:** compliance effort drops from days and weeks to minutes and hours — and the audit trail that regulators demand becomes a byproduct of how you work, not a project you fund.

**Current status:** A prototype has proven a unified knowledge graph capable of handling multiple EU regulation (CRA, GDPR, NIS2). Actively prototyping Query mechanism for answerings free text questions about regulatory and policy topics.
